BrisaSync is an open-source Linux desktop application for bidirectional synchronization between user-selected Google Drive folders and local folders. The drive scope is required to discover existing folders, enumerate their contents, download and upload files, and propagate updates, renames and deletions within the configured folder pairs. drive.file is insufficient for discovering and continuously synchronizing arbitrary pre-existing folder trees and files added outside the app; read-only scopes cannot support uploads or changes. openid and userinfo.email identify the connected account and display its email in the interface. Drive data and OAuth tokens are handled locally on the user's computer, with direct communication to Google APIs and no developer-operated intermediary server.
